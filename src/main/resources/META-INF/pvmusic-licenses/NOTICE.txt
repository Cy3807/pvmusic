  Source: https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/com/tianscar/javasound/jaad/0.9.4/jaad-0.9.4-sources.jar
- JLayer 1.0.1, LGPL: https://github.com/umjammer/jlayer
- jaudiotagger 3.0.1, LGPL-2.1-or-later: https://bitbucket.org/ijabz/jaudiotagger
  Source: https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/net/jthink/jaudiotagger/3.0.1/jaudiotagger-3.0.1-sources.jar
